261 * polyeta_unpack produces coefficients in [-MLDSA_ETA, MLDSA_ETA] for
262 * well-formed inputs (i.e., those produced by polyeta_pack).
263 * However, when passed an arbitrary byte array, it may produce smaller values,

266 * inputs in the CBMC proofs.
